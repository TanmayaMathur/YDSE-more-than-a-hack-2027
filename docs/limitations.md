# Limitations and unresolved information

- A valid CSV establishes schema correctness, not possession accuracy. Private
  full-match spellF1 and comparative ranking are unavailable.
- The candidate is heuristic and has no independent real-match calibration.
  Static-ball and quiet-player patterns do not perfectly identify referee decisions.
- Long ball gaps retain only temporal/tactical context. The model cannot know
  every turnover during an occlusion, and no exact position is invented for it.
- Motion localization accepted seven restart windows; the others remain uncertain.
- Anonymous slots limit stable player-specific kinematics. Jump rejection cannot
  resolve all identity switches or duplicate players.
- No ball height, broadcast alignment, optical flow or video-based recovery is used.
- Synthetic scenarios are simplified and do not estimate real-match quality.
- Source review covered seven developed implementations. The broad inventory has
  many metadata-only leads; private/unindexed code and unavailable PRs were not read.
- Signed participation terms were not found. The team must verify them and the
  precise deadline/form link in organiser communications. The Drive invitation
  is not treated as disclosure permission.
- A public GitHub URL and competition upload await the team's approval and action.
- November 16 and submission-day update scans are still future manual steps.
