# Data integrity

Eight archives were extracted into separate checksum-qualified directories. All member paths, duplicates and expansion limits were checked; CRCs verified during extraction. Nine expected assets identified by name and format, SHA-256 recorded locally. Tracking and homography counts and contiguous frame IDs verified for both sources. Both videos passed MP4 signature checks; full video decoding was not performed. Canonical files are hard links: inference opens them read-only. Originals remain untouched.

Detailed inventories and hashes: private/archive_manifest.json and private/asset_manifest.json. Signed participation terms were not supplied; local-only processing follows the public restrictions. No match content was sent to a hosted model or external service.
